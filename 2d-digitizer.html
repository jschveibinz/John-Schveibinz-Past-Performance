Hardware 2D Digitizer and Storage System Prototype

Acousto-Optic Signal Processing Hardware

OrganizationSystems Engineering and Development Corporation (SEDC)
Work period1985–1990
ContributorJohn Schveibinz

Product overview

A two-dimensional hardware digitizer and storage prototype for a government acousto-optic signal processing system.

Work performed

Designed and developed the hardware, including schematics, board design, chip selection and placement, testing, and delivery of the prototype.

Hardware design; board engineering; prototype testing and delivery

Engineering significance

Provided the digitization and storage hardware needed within a larger acousto-optic processing system, connecting component-level design with system integration and prototype delivery.
