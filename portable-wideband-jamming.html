Product overview

A portable wideband communications signal jamming system developed for a government customer.

Work performed

Personally designed and developed the system, including specification, integration, and testing of high-power RF amplifiers, wideband RF switches, software control, and the user interface. Designed mechanical packaging for thermal resilience.

System design; RF integration and testing; software controls; mechanical packaging

Engineering significance

Integrated RF hardware, operator controls, and thermal packaging into a portable system. The source reports successful sales without providing quantitative performance or sales data.
