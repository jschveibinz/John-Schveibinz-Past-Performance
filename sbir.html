SBIR research and commercialization

Proposal development, engineering interfaces, and the transition from research to practical products.

Author-reported contributions

John Schveibinz reports co-authoring nine SBIR proposals. His retrospective specifically describes assistance with the U.S. Navy SPAWAR Phase I proposal for hyperspectral material detection and classification, followed by writing the successful Phase II proposal, including commercialization strategy and commercial partnership development. Joseph C. Harsanyi served as principal investigator.

Related work included establishing ASIT’s systems engineering and proposal preparation processes, writing MATLAB and Visual Basic interfaces to Harsanyi’s algorithms, and participating in hyperspectral product design.

Public records

  • ASIT’s official SBIR portfolio lists nine Phase I and three Phase II awards. These are company award counts, distinct from the author’s reported nine proposal contributions.
  • 1996 Navy Phase II award identifies hyperspectral material detection research and Joseph C. Harsanyi as principal investigator.
  • 2003 Direction Finding (DF) Module award identifies Joseph C. Harsanyi as principal investigator and John J. Schveibinz as business contact.
  • Department of the Navy SBIR/STTR Successes (2001), printed pp. 70–71 describes the spectral processing program, names Prospectre among its outcomes, and lists John as company contact (with his surname misspelled in the publication).

Evidence boundaries

Public award and program records support the company’s research history. They do not list proposal co-authors or independently establish authorship of nine proposals. The nine-proposal statement and individual work descriptions derive from the author’s account. A full proposal-by-proposal list has not been reconstructed.

Public records checked October 30, 2026. The author-supplied retrospective is unpublished and is not reproduced on this site.
