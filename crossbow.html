Crossbow

Multi-Dimensional Signal Processing and Synthesis Fortran Software Suite

OrganizationSystems Engineering and Development Corporation (SEDC)
Work period1985–1990
ContributorJohn Schveibinz

Product overview

A Fortran software suite for synthesizing complex modulated waveforms and analyzing them through multidimensional, quadratic signal processing. Crossbow ran on VAX/VMS computers with VT220 terminals.

Work performed

Contributed to waveform synthesis; Wigner, cyclostationary, product-plane, cross-ambiguity-function, and cepstrum analyses; and the storage, transformations, extraction, printing, and export functions needed to produce analytical results. Developed algorithms using Crossbow and presented results to customers.

Software development; algorithm development; customer presentations

Engineering significance

Combined signal synthesis, complementary analysis representations, and data handling in a common engineering environment for investigating signal characteristics.
