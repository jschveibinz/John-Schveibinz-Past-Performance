Airborne Surveillance System

RF Collection, Analysis, and Geolocation

OrganizationCalifornia Microwave
Work period1991–1996
ContributorJohn Schveibinz

Product overview

An airborne surveillance system integrating RF collection and analysis, RF geolocation, satellite backhaul, remote control, image collection and analysis, and user interfaces.

Work performed

Served as lead designer and systems engineer for the proposal and awarded project. Led more than 15 engineers and technical staff across RF, electrical, software, mechanical, and signal-analysis disciplines; managed major vendor relationships and subcontracts. Guided design, calibration, testing, and deployment.

Lead designer; systems engineering; multidisciplinary team and subcontract leadership

Engineering significance

Developed remote engineering access over a private wireless bridge, modified wireless-supported calibration procedures, and UNIX software architectures with inter-process communications. Addressed antenna-interface corrosion, aircraft thermal integration, RF interference, and engineering review gates.
