Mobile Direction Finding Hardware Prototypes

Prototype Development and Transition to Production

OrganizationApplied Signal and Image Technology (ASIT)
Work period1996–2009
ContributorJohn Schveibinz

Product overview

Prototype hardware for testing mobile direction finding concepts against modern signal types, followed by continued development and commercialization of mobile DF systems.

Work performed

Designed and developed the initial prototype hardware and continued the engineering work through the transition to production and sales.

Hardware design; prototype development; production transition

Engineering significance

The author reports responsibility for successful production and sales of thousands of mobile DF systems. Public SBIR records separately document ASIT’s DF research; they do not independently verify this sales quantity or establish that a particular award funded these prototypes.
